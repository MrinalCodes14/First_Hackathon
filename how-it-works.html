How CivicDrishti Works

Reporting a civic issue takes just a few simple steps.

1

📸 Spot an Issue

Find a civic problem such as a pothole, garbage, broken streetlight, water leakage or road damage.

2

📝 Report It

Choose the issue category, describe the problem, add a photo and provide the location.

3

📍 Track It

View your submitted reports and track their current status from the My Reports page.
